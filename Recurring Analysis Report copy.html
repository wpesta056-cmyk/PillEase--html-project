The U.S. Chronic Prescription Landscape

As of 2025, an estimated 168 million Americans — approximately 50.1% of the total population — are actively taking at least one recurring prescription medication for three months or longer. This report synthesizes population health data, pharmaceutical fill volumes, packaging economics, and adherence metrics to provide a comprehensive view of the chronic medication market.

Section 05

Medication Adherence & Healthcare Costs

Non-adherence to chronic prescription medications represents one of the most costly and preventable crises in U.S. healthcare. The financial and human toll is staggering — and the shift to mail-order pharmacy models offers a measurable pathway to improvement.

30-Day Retail Fill — Adherence Rate
~55%
